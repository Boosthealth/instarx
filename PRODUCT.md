# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults considering doctor-prescribed GLP-1 weight-loss treatment, often arriving from paid acquisition on mobile. They need to understand the treatment timeline, compare transparent pricing, establish trust in the clinical process, and decide whether to complete the medical intake.

## Product Purpose

InstaRx marketing pages explain accessible, clinician-guided weight-loss treatment and convert qualified visitors into the intake flow. Success means that visitors understand the offer, its renewal terms, inclusions, expected timeline, and medical limitations before choosing a treatment plan.

## Brand Personality

Reassuring, candid, and motivating. The experience should feel clinically responsible without becoming cold, and conversion-focused without feeling coercive.

## Anti-references

- Generic pharmaceutical pages that bury pricing or care details.
- Aggressive direct-response pages that manufacture urgency or obscure subscription terms.
- Unbranded template layouts that ignore the established InstaRx visual language.
- Interfaces that depend on motion, color alone, or tiny legal copy to communicate essential information.

## Design Principles

- Make the offer understandable before asking for commitment.
- Build confidence through transparent pricing, real member stories, and precise clinical language.
- Use momentum as the narrative: explain why sustained treatment matters, then connect the fourth-month offer to that journey.
- Preserve the recognizable InstaRx voice and interaction patterns across landing pages and intake handoff.
- Treat accessibility, performance, and attribution continuity as conversion requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic controls, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, screen-reader labels, touch targets of at least 44px, and mobile layouts without horizontal overflow.
