The Part Of An Erection Nobody Explains To You

Here is what I did not know at 51, and what no doctor has ever spent two minutes explaining to me at the point of writing a prescription.

An erection runs on two systems, not one. There is the plumbing, which is blood flow. And there is the signal that starts the whole thing, which is not blood flow at all. It is dopamine, firing in a part of your brain called the hypothalamus, and it is the difference between an erection happening and an erection being wanted.

Viagra, Cialis, Levitra, the chewables, the mints, the generic stuff. Every single one of them is a PDE5 inhibitor, and every single one of them works on the plumbing. That is not a criticism. On the plumbing they are excellent. It is just the boundary of what that class of drug does.

Which means if your gap is on the signal side, a stronger blue pill is a bigger dose of the wrong answer. You can double it and it will not reach the thing that is actually missing. I know, because I tried that for about eight months.

So Why Does Everyone Only Sell You The Plumbing?

Because the molecule that works on the other half is old.

It is called apomorphine. It has nothing to do with Viagra, it is not a stimulant, and it was studied seriously for exactly this in the early 2000s. The pharmacology held up. Then it went nowhere, and the reason has nothing to do with whether it works: it was off-patent. There was no branded product to build a business around, so nobody built one.

It sat on a shelf for twenty years while the entire consumer ED market got built on blood flow alone. That is the whole story. Not a conspiracy, just a patent expiring.

And Then There Is The Forty-Five Minute Problem

Separate issue, same evening. The blue pill wants about forty-five minutes of notice, and your life does not give you forty-five minutes of notice.

So you start scheduling. You decide in the afternoon that tonight is on. You time dinner. You turn down the second drink and feel like an idiot about it. You disappear for five minutes and come back. And if the evening does not go anywhere, you have now had a whole failed project instead of a quiet night in.

That is the part that actually damages a marriage, and it is not caused by the erection. It is caused by the hour in front of it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast does it actually work?
Sublingual absorption skips first-pass liver metabolism, which is what accounts for the difference against a tablet you swallow, and onset is measured in minutes rather than most of an hour. Individual response varies. The number that matters is not the published figure, it is whether it is short enough that you stop planning around it.
I have tried everything. Why would this be different?
If everything you have tried was a PDE5 inhibitor, and the blue pill, the weekend pill, the chewables, the mints and generic sildenafil all are, then you have tried several versions of one mechanism. The question is which half your gap sits on. If it is the arousal half, nothing you have taken has addressed it.
Is it FDA-approved?
The individual active ingredients are FDA-approved medicines. The compounded combination is not approved as a combination product, and the FDA does not verify the safety or effectiveness of compounded drugs. Any page in this category that tells you otherwise is misleading you, and it is worth knowing before you buy from anybody, us included.
Do I build a tolerance to it?
PDE5 inhibitors are not stimulants and not hormones, and the mechanism is not known to deplete with use. Raise long-term use on the intake so the prescribing physician can answer it for your situation rather than in general.
What if my wife asks what it is?
Mine asked. I told her. It turned out she had been waiting a year for me to bring it up, which I suspect is more common than anyone admits. The packaging says nothing on the outside if you would rather have that conversation in your own time.
What if the doctor says no?
Then you are not charged, and you have learned something useful for free. A no usually means something in your history needs a different approach, which is worth knowing.
